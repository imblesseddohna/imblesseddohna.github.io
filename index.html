About me

Hi, I’m Dohna, a software developer and technical trainer from Trivandrum, Kerala. I graduated in Computer Science in 2021, started out as a software trainee, and have been building and teaching software ever since.

Today I work as Software Developer and Technical Head at Netics IT Innovations, where I build websites and applications, lead a team, and train students and working professionals in full-stack development and test automation. I also give guest lectures at engineering colleges.

I enjoy taking a project from design to hosting to maintenance, and helping other people learn to do the same. Right now I’m learning cloud, starting with AWS.
